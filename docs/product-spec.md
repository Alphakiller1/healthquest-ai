# Product spec

HealthQuest helps adults understand everyday food, movement, sleep, and habits, and prepare questions for a clinician. The first audience is adults 18+ in the United States who may have limited access to care.

The product is general wellness education. It does not diagnose, prescribe, change treatment, interpret drug interactions, or handle emergencies.

Four systems stay separate:

- Education: curated claims tied to source records
- Context: which of those claims fit the user’s voluntary goals
- Engagement: quests and points from server rules
- Safety: deterministic blocks before any model call

The model writes language only. It does not invent nutrients, citations, points, or emergency instructions.

Out of scope until a later phase: wearables, labs, grocery prices, photo recognition, voice, community, family accounts, and genetic data.
